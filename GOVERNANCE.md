# Governance

Nyxdoc is an independent open-source project.

Seungji Lee is the project lead, primary maintainer, and final decision maker
for product direction, releases, repository policy, and use of the Nyxdoc
brand. Contributors are encouraged to propose and discuss changes openly.

OpenAI Codex is used as a development collaborator. It is not a legal owner,
maintainer, or decision-making authority.

The governance model may evolve if the maintainer community grows. Material
changes will be documented in this file.
