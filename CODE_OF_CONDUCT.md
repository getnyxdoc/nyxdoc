# Code of Conduct

Nyxdoc welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and
points of view.

When participating:

- be respectful and discuss ideas constructively;
- do not discriminate, harass, threaten, or demean others;
- do not publish another person's private information, credentials, documents,
  or identifying data; and
- assume good intent while remaining precise about technical risks.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community
and the project. The project lead has the final decision on project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@nyxdoc.com`.
